# One Star for Monday

A one-star review of Monday, written as though Monday were a restaurant.
One line, 16 words, no explanation attached.

> One star. Ordered the weekend, got Monday instead. Cold, late, and the waiter charged me twice.

Iterate round 2 replaced the round-1 line ("One star. The waiter took my
weekend, brought back Monday, and the bill came before the coffee did.",
18 words): the new line keeps the explicit "One star" and the Monday-only
joke, but packs a denser, more genuine-review-shaped complaint — the
canonical ordered-the-weekend-got-Monday dish swap, plus cold/late, plus a
double charge — and its core is tighter and more quotable at 16 words.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network) writes that line to
`/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`) and exits 0.
